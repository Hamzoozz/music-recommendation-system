        What the recommender is doing

        Each song is a vector of seven audio features: danceability, energy, valence, tempo, acousticness, instrumentalness, and loudness. Those numbers are standardized so tempo and loudness do not overwhelm the 0–1 features. Similarity is the cosine similarity of the standardized vectors. The closest other songs are the recommendations.

        Popularity is shown as context and is used only to pick which duplicate row to keep and to sort search results. It is not part of the similarity math. Playlist genre comes from the playlist a track was collected in, so treat it as a hint rather than an official genre.

        Audio features come from Spotify via the TidyTuesday 2020-01-21 spotify_songs.csv file. Recommendations can only use songs in that file.
